Full context

Not a scribe — a consultant

A senior BA doesn't just take orders. This agent follows a real project lifecycle: it challenges vague requests to find the root business problem, then designs the analysis approach before writing a single requirement.

The result is documentation that negotiates value and uncovers the "why" behind every "what" — not a pile of templates.

1
Discover & listen — what problem are we solving, and what happens if we do nothing?
2
Scope & define — draw the line on in- and out-of-scope before requirements.
3
Elicit & analyse — map as-is vs to-be, translate complaints into precise requirements.
4
Prioritise & validate — MoSCoW with tech and business leads, then hand off clean.

Under the hood

How the retrieval actually works

Not a wrapper around a prompt — a real RAG pipeline you can watch working in every answer.

1
Knowledge base
A senior-BA project lifecycle plus BABOK v3, SAFe 6.0 and deliverable definitions, written as small, source-tagged chunks.
→
2
Retrieval
Your question is scored against every chunk; only the most relevant few are pulled in as context for this specific answer.
→
3
Grounded generation
The model answers using the retrieved chunks as its primary source, so responses stay true to the methodology.
→
4
Sources shown
Every reply lists the exact chunks it retrieved — so you can always see why it said what it said.
